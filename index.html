Why This Room, Why Now

Are your district's AI pilots, tools, and investments adding up to a clear direction—or do they need to be connected by a shared plan? On September 16–17 in Renton, Eddi, WSIPC, and WSPEF are bringing together a small group of Washington district leaders to build that plan together.

We're keeping the room intentionally small, and we'll lock in the venue once we have a clear headcount. Your early registration is what lets us move forward.

There's no cost to attend. Your cooperative exists to help you use your data, and this is the next step in that journey. If you're able, bring a colleague or two to carry the work home.

What You'll Walk Away With

Two focused days, built entirely around your district's own priorities.

1

Clarity on agentic AI

A clear picture of what agentic AI actually is, and how it differs from the tools you've already tried.

2

Build-vs-buy framework

A practical framework for deciding where to build, where to buy, and where to wait.

3

A draft roadmap

A draft roadmap built around your own district's priorities — not a generic template.

4

A peer network

A network of Washington leaders working through the same questions, in the same room.
